# KAIROS

## Meaning
"Kairos" is an ancient Greek concept referring to the right, opportune, or decisive moment. That concept is central to the product.

KAIROS helps users determine:
- *What should I say?*
- *How should I say it?*
- *What format should I use?*
- *Why is this the right moment?*

## Product Positioning
KAIROS is a **personal content strategist**. 

It takes product information, target audience, platform, tone, user requirements, trends, meme formats, and previous user feedback to produce:
- Hooks
- Content angles
- Memes
- Dialogues
- Short-form scripts
- Shot plans
- Captions
- Hashtags

It learns the user's creative preferences through feedback.

## What KAIROS is NOT
- An AI chatbot
- A ChatGPT clone
- A generic AI writing tool
- A caption generator
- A social media scheduler
- An analytics dashboard
- An "AI marketing platform"

**KAIROS should feel like a creative strategist that understands your product and the culture around it.**

## Brand Promise
**Primary:** "Say the right thing at the right moment."

**Supporting concepts:**
- "Turn products into culture."
- "Make your product worth talking about."
- "Find your angle."

*Note: Keep copy concise. Do not fill the interface with marketing slogans.*

## Brand Personality
**KAIROS should feel:**
- Intelligent
- Cultured
- Human
- Editorial
- Confident
- Contemporary
- Slightly irreverent
- Curious
- Socially aware
- Restrained

**It should NOT feel:**
- Corporate
- Robotic
- Futuristic for no reason
- Overly technical
- Generic SaaS
- Loud
- Childish
- "AI-generated"

## Design Register
**Primary direction:** EDITORIAL CREATIVE STUDIO × MODERN CREATOR TOOL

**Additional inspiration:**
- Independent creative studios
- Contemporary magazines
- Publishing tools
- Premium writing products
- Creator software
- Apple-level restraint
- Cultural publications

*The interface should look deliberately art-directed. It should NOT look assembled from a generic component library.*

## Content Philosophy
**Generated content is the actual product.**
The result page should NOT be a collection of generic cards. 

**Hierarchy:**
1. Hook (Dominates visually)
2. Content angle
3. Meme (Should feel like actual media)
4. Dialogue
5. Reel blueprint (Script should feel like a practical shooting document)
6. Caption

## Personalization Philosophy
KAIROS learns user preferences through feedback. The interface should communicate this naturally.
- **Do say:** "Got it. I'll lean more sarcastic next time." or "Preference saved."
- **Do NOT say:** "Model retraining completed."
*This is preference memory, not model fine-tuning.*
